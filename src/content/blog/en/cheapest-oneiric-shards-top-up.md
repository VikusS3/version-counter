## Oneiric Shards pricing in plain terms

Shard packs are priced in fixed tiers, and the larger the pack the better the ratio of shards per currency unit. On top of that, the first top up bonus doubles every pack one time.

## Safety checklist

1. **Never share your password.** Legitimate sellers deliver a code or balance, never a login.
2. **Match the region.** The card must match the country settings of the store your account uses, not your physical location.
3. **Instant digital delivery.** Delays after payment on a digital code are a warning sign.
4. **Real transaction history**, not just a landing page with a logo.
5. **Buyer protection on the payment method.** Card or PayPal — never wire transfer or crypto to an individual.

Follow all five and the worst realistic outcome is a code that does not redeem, which you can refund.

## Region traps specific to Star Rail

Star Rail accounts are global, but the store balance you pay with is not. The common failures:

- Google Play balance from a country your Google account is not set to.
- App Store card from a region that does not match the Apple ID.
- Console wallet cards bought for the wrong PSN or Xbox region.

Check the region printed in the listing **before** paying, and keep the receipt until the code is redeemed.

## FAQ

### Is it legal to buy Oneiric Shards through a discounted gift card?

Yes. You redeem the card yourself and make a normal in-game purchase, which HoYoverse processes as usual.

### Can I get banned?

Not for using discounted gift cards. Bans in this area come from account sharing, chargeback fraud, or buying from sellers using stolen payment methods.

### What happens if the card is for the wrong region?

It will not redeem. That is a wasted purchase, not a lost account — which is exactly why the region check comes before payment, not after.

### Does the first top up bonus ever come back?

It resets on some anniversary-style events, but you should not plan around it. Treat it as a one-time multiplier and use it deliberately.

### Where do I see my remaining bonus?

In the in-game purchase screen, where each pack shows its bonus state before you confirm.
