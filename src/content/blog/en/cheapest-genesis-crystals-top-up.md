## Why Genesis Crystals cost what they cost

HoYoverse does not discount Genesis Crystals directly. The price of every pack is fixed, the same for every player, and identical across regions once you convert currency. The only in-game discount that exists is the **first top up bonus**, which doubles the crystals on each pack one single time per account.

That means the only realistic way to pay less is to change how you pay for the pack — usually by buying the payment method itself below face value.

## Is buying discounted gift cards for Genesis Crystals safe?

Buying a gift card is safe. Redeeming it in your own account is safe. The crystals are then delivered by HoYoverse itself, through the normal in-game purchase flow, so there is nothing third-party touching your account.

Use this checklist before you buy:

1. **You never share your password.** Not for a "cheaper top up," not for a "verification" step. Legitimate sellers deliver a code or a balance, never a login.
2. **The card matches your account region.** A US iTunes card will not fund a European Apple ID. Region mismatch is the single most common reason a purchase "fails."
3. **The code is delivered digitally and instantly.** Long waits after payment are a warning sign.
4. **The seller has real transaction history and reviews**, not just a landing page.
5. **You pay with a method that has chargeback protection.** Card or PayPal, never a bank transfer or crypto to a stranger.

## Region and server risks to know

Your **server region** (America, Europe, Asia, TW/HK/MO) and your **store region** are separate things. Your crystals are tied to the account, but the gift card you fund the purchase with must match the country settings of the store your account logs into.

Typical mistakes:

- Redeeming a card from the wrong country and getting an "invalid code" error.
- Buying a PlayStation card for a different region than your PSN account.
- Using a card bought in a currency your account region does not accept.

Fix: check the region printed on the listing **before** paying, and keep the receipt until the code is redeemed.

## FAQ

### Is it legal to buy Genesis Crystals through a discounted gift card?

Yes. You are buying a normal gift card, redeeming it yourself, and making a standard in-game purchase. HoYoverse processes the transaction as usual.

### Why is the first top up bonus important?

It doubles the crystals on each pack exactly once. Spending a discounted gift card on a pack you have already topped up wastes that bonus, so time your purchase before you use it.

### Can I get banned for buying cheaper crystals?

Not for using discounted gift cards. Bans in this space come from account sharing, chargeback fraud, or buying from sellers who use stolen payment methods — none of which apply when you redeem the card yourself.

### What is the cheapest pack per dollar?

Larger packs give more crystals per dollar than small ones, and the first top up bonus doubles that ratio. If you are buying a big amount, one large pack beats several small ones.

### Do prices change?

Gift card discounts change constantly with currency rates and regional promotions. Always compare the live listing price against face value before buying.
