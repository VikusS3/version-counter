> **Version 1.5 — "Dreamscape of Wind and Snow"**
>
> Last updated: **September 22, 2026**

Arknights: Endfield Version 1.5, **"Dreamscape of Wind and Snow,"** is now live after maintenance on **September 2, 2026** (Asia UTC+8; Americas/Europe content live after September 1, 2026 23:00 UTC-5). The update introduces the new 6★ operator **Typhoeus** on the **Winter Hunt** Chartered Headhunting banner, plus the free 5★ operator **Purrchena**.

Version 1.5 also adds new areas in **Wuling**, a Suiming time-loop puzzle city, new story missions, enemies, systems improvements, limited-time events, and free rewards.

## ⚡ Typhoeus — 6★ Nature Striker

Typhoeus is the headline new operator of Version 1.5 and the rate-up on **Winter Hunt**.

She wields a **bow as tall as she is** and is classified as a **Nature Striker** (Arts Unit). Per the GRYPHLINE press release, she can **leap into the air** for airborne attacks, hit **multiple targets**, evade ground attacks, slow enemies with **Barrage Array**, and deal damage with **Nature Burst**.

## 💬 How to Redeem Arknights: Endfield Codes

Codes are **redeemed in-game only**. There is no official web redemption URL for Arknights: Endfield (per secondary sources as of September 22, 2026).

### In-Game

1. Open **Arknights: Endfield**.
2. Open the main menu (**Menu**).
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Open the **Platform / Account** tab.
5. Select **Exchange Code**.
6. Enter the code and confirm.
7. Claim the rewards from your in-game **Mail**.

> **Note:** The **Mail** system unlocks during the **[Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege]** main mission.

> **Tip:** Codes are case-sensitive. Redeem them as soon as possible because some codes have short expiration windows. `ENDFIELD4PC` can only be redeemed on the **PC client**.

### Who is the new operator in Endfield 1.5?

- **Typhoeus** — 6★ Nature Striker (Winter Hunt rate-up)
