> **Version 1.6 — "Sanctuary of Ink"**
>
> Last updated: **October 10, 2026**

Arknights: Endfield Version 1.6, **"Sanctuary of Ink,"** launches on **October 15, 2026** after the version update and maintenance (server time; Asia UTC+8, Americas/Europe UTC-5). The update is the next **Core Chapter Version** and was detailed in the official Special Program that aired on **October 6, 2026 at 19:30 (UTC+8)**. It adds the new 6★ operator **Si** on the **Rejuvenation** Chartered Headhunting banner, the 6★ **Argent Flow** in Phase 2, and a **Tangtang** RE-Factor rerun.

Version 1.6 also launches the game on **Steam** the same day, with cross-platform account sync, and adds the new **Si's Painting** region, an inn-management mode, new events, Contingency Contract content, and free rewards.

## 💬 How to Redeem Arknights: Endfield Codes

Codes are **redeemed in-game only**. There is no official web redemption URL for Arknights: Endfield.

### In-Game

1. Open **Arknights: Endfield**.
2. Open the main menu (**Menu**).
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Open the **Platform / Account** tab.
5. Select **Exchange Code**.
6. Enter the code and confirm.
7. Claim the rewards from your in-game **Mail**.

> **Note:** The **Mail** system unlocks during the **[Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege]** main mission.

> **Tip:** Codes are case-sensitive. Redeem them as soon as possible because some codes have short expiration windows. `ENDFIELD4PC` can only be redeemed on the **PC client**.

### New Version Guide Web Rewards (Not a Code)

The New Version Guide rewards are claimed on the **official website page**, not in-game:

1. Open the official **[Sanctuary of Ink] New Version Guide** page (linked in the official announcement).
2. Log in with your game account.
3. Make sure character creation is complete on that account.
4. Click **Claim Rewards** before the Version 1.6 maintenance begins.
5. Rewards arrive via in-game mail.
