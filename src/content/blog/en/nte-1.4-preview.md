> **Version 1.4 — "For Whom the Verses Mourn"**
>
> Preview last verified: **September 24, 2026**

Neverness to Everness Version 1.4, **"For Whom the Verses Mourn,"** launches on **September 30, 2026**. The update introduces two new Limited S-Class Characters, **Blackbird** and **Akane Rin**, plus a **Lacrimosa** rerun.

Version 1.4 also opens **Pukaland**, adds the St. Arbor story location, delivers new main and spin-off stories, introduces new activities and Anomaly Commissions, expands Porsche Collaboration Vol. 2, and adds a new accessory category.

Blackbird is the first new Limited S-Class of Version 1.4. She is a **Psyche Main DPS** associated with Instant Cycle and DMG Boost mechanics.

### Confirmed Kit Summary

- Her basic attacks create a multi-hit Psyche combo.
- Her skill applies a **Forsaken Feather** mark to targets.
- When marked targets are attacked by other party members, Blackbird can trigger a coordinated AoE attack.
- Her Ultimate, **Requies Animarum**, transforms her into the Witch of Deliverance, deals major Psyche damage, and increases the party's Attack.
- In the open world, Blackbird can transform into a bird and fly.

- A field version of the skill can distribute enhanced attacks across nearby enemies.

## Version 1.4 Story Update

### The Witch

The first-half main story follows Blackbird after a mysterious request leads her away from home. The story moves into **St. Arbor**, a Location Anomaly built around an old village, a central burning tree, and a witch-hunt setting.

### To Us, Back Then

The second-half spin-off story, **"To Us, Back Then,"** focuses on Akane Rin's past. It continues the storyline involving The Whoots and the StarSign live house, and introduces a performance connected to Clement Academy.

## New Locations and Anomaly Commissions

### Pukaland

Pukaland is the new island amusement park opening with Version 1.4. Its main areas include entrance attractions, a stamina-based maze, a first-person haunted house, a chocolate-volcano roller coaster, and a castle with an illusion fireworks show.

The **Pukaland Travelogue** tracks completed attractions and rewards. Puka Stars can be exchanged through the event system for souvenirs and other items.

## Free Rewards

### Solid Dice ×20

Version 1.4 is reported to provide **Solid Dice ×20** in total:

- **Solid Dice ×10** through the seven-day Circle Gifts login event.
- **Solid Dice ×10** through the Pukaland Fest Special Gift Pack after the September 30 update.

The version gift also includes the **Like Clockwork** avatar frame. After the expected October 21 update, the second gift delivery includes **Fons ×2,000,000** and Akane Rin's **House Special** outfit.

### Puka Membership Program

The Puka Membership Program unlocks through City Tycoon at Level 16. Its reported reward track includes:

- S-Class Standard Character Selection Box ×2
- S-Class Standard Arc Selection Box ×1
- Character outfits ×3
- Accessories and other City Tycoon rewards

The two standard Character Selection Boxes are the version's largest confirmed free character progression reward.

## Neverness to Everness 1.4 Redeem Codes

> **Last checked: September 24, 2026**
>
> No Version 1.4 redeem code was verified as active on this date. The three livestream codes expired on **September 20, 2026 at 23:59 (UTC+8)**.

| Code           | Status  |
| -------------- | ------- |
| `WITCHHOUSE`   | Expired |
| `THEWHOOTS`    | Expired |
| `PUKALANDGOGO` | Expired |

Some sources spell the first code as `WITCHOUSE`. Because the livestream codes have expired and reported reward quantities conflict between sources, neither spelling should be presented as an active code.

Codes are redeemed in-game:

1. Open Neverness to Everness.
2. Open the phone menu.
3. Select the three-dot menu.
4. Choose **Redeem Code**.
5. Enter the code and claim the rewards from in-game mail.

Check back after launch or Phase 2 for newly announced codes, and verify them in-game before relying on a code.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does Neverness to Everness 1.4 release?

Version 1.4 launches on **September 30, 2026**. The exact maintenance window and server start time have not been confirmed in the official English written notice at the time of this preview.

### When does Version 1.4 end?

The longest dated content, including Pukaland Travelogue and Akane Rin's phase, is expected to end on **November 11, 2026**. The final maintenance and Version 1.5 notice remain pending.

### Who are the new Characters in Version 1.4?

The new Limited S-Class Characters are:

- **Blackbird** — Psyche Main DPS, first half
- **Akane Rin** — Lakshana Main DPS, second half

### Who is rerunning in Version 1.4?

**Lacrimosa** returns during the first half alongside Blackbird.

### How many free pulls does Version 1.4 provide?

Version 1.4 is reported to provide **Solid Dice ×20** across Circle Gifts and the Pukaland Fest Special Gift Pack.

### Are the Version 1.4 redeem codes still active?

No. `WITCHHOUSE`, `THEWHOOTS`, and `PUKALANDGOGO` expired on September 20, 2026. No replacement 1.4 code was verified active on September 24.

### Are Blackbird and Akane Rin builds available?

Only their core kits are confirmed. Verified Arc builds, Modules, stat priorities, rotations, and team compositions remain pending until the Characters can be tested after launch.

### What is new in Pukaland?

Pukaland adds an explorable amusement-park island, four themed areas, first-person attractions, mini-games, event rewards, a new vehicle and glider, and access to the Puka Membership Program.
