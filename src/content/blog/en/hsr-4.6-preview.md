# Honkai: Star Rail 4.6: Banners, Events, Builds & Redeem Codes

> **Version 4.6 — "Dance With the Beast Before Moonrise"**
>
> Last updated: **September 22, 2026**

Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.6, **"Dance With the Beast Before Moonrise,"** is the next update, announced in the official Special Program livestream on **September 20, 2026**. The headline addition is the new limited 5★ character **Pearl** (Ice / Elation), alongside reruns for **Evanescia** in Phase 1 and **Mortenax Blade** in Phase 2.

Version 4.6 also brings a new Trailblaze Mission, the **Life Sciences Institute** map in Astropolis, two new Relic sets, a new boss, limited-time events, free login rewards, and new outfits for **Evanescia** and **Hyacine**.

# ⚡ Pearl — New 5★ Ice Elation

Pearl is the main addition in Version 4.6 and the only new playable character confirmed for the patch.

She is an **IPC Ten Stonehearts** member and CEO of Planarcadia. Her kit centers on **Certified Banger**: her Talent consumes it to block ally damage, her Skill grants allies extra Elation DMG that scales with the number of Elation teammates, and her Ultimate designates an ally as the "Aesthetic Archetype" and advances their action. Enhanced Basic ATKs in Deep Learning heal and cleanse the team, and her Technique generates Certified Banger.

*Kit details are derived from the official Special Program via secondary coverage and may change with final patch notes.*

# 💻 How to Redeem HSR Codes

You can redeem Honkai: Star Rail codes either in-game or through HoYoverse's official redemption page.

## In-Game

1. Open **Honkai: Star Rail**.
2. Open the phone menu.
3. Select **Redemption Code**.
4. Enter the code.
5. Open your in-game mailbox to claim the rewards.

## Official Website

Use the official HoYoverse redemption page:

https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/gift

> **Tip:** Codes are case-sensitive. Redeem them as soon as possible because some codes have short expiration windows — livestream codes often expire within 24 hours.
